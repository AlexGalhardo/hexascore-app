behavior: `Submit` takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the target, then rejects a live `catalog_locks` row with 423 `field-locked` (`lockReason`), an open same-author field or an identical `after` with 409 `suggestion-duplicate` (`existingId` only for the author's own), and a scalar `before` that does not match the catalogue with 409 `stale-before` (`field`, `current`). The current release date is `{"value","precision"}` (or null). Titles, synopses, external ids and covers are read for items, editions and entities. A different `after` from another author is kept and both rows get `meta.competing`. Expired locks are ignored, not deleted. A 409 or 423 rolls the transaction back, so the draft stays a draft and the daily quota is not consumed.
