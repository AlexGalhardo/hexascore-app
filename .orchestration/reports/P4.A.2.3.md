behavior: `contributor_stats.accuracy` is the generated Laplace column `(approved+1)/(approved+rejected+reverted+2)`. `RebuildStats` rewrites the counters from suggestion rows: submitted when `submitted_at` is set, approved when the row reached `applied` or `reverted`, rejected except `duplicate`/`stale`/`target_gone`, reverted only for reason `incorrect`, withdrawn for that status. Tier is `blocked` at decided ≥ 20 and accuracy < 0.20 for 30 days, `low` at decided ≥ 10 and accuracy < 0.40, `new` at zero applied, otherwise `standard`. A future `blocked_until` is kept, so an admin block is not cleared. `trusted_eligible` is approved ≥ 50, accuracy ≥ 0.90, and no `spam` rejection in 90 days. It is stored and read by nothing. Three `incorrect`/`no_source`/`bad_source`/`policy`/`spam` rejections in 24 h set `cooldown_until` 48 h after the third. Any `spam` sets it 7 days after that decision. The later end wins. `Submit` then returns 429 `suggestion-cooldown` with `cooldownUntil` and `Retry-After`, and the draft stays a draft. The hourly job expires drafts idle for 14 days, deletes an uploaded `community/covers/…` key, leaves an https cover, releases `in_review` claims idle for 2 h, and rebuilds stats. A second rebuild of an already correct row does not change it. `community:stats:rebuild` calls that function. The command lives in `app/console` because that is where `vukem make:command` registers commands.

risks: No new HTTP route. Honeypot and burst limits stay in P4.A.2.4. Cover bytes are deleted only when the change `after` is a `community/covers/` key. Do not push.
