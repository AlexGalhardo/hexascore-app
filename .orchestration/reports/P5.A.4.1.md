## What changed

- `src/query/client.ts`: `createQueryClient()` (`staleTime` 30 s, `retry` 1); `wireQueryManagers()` feeds `focusManager` from `src/platform/appState.ts` (new, AppState) and `onlineManager` from `src/platform/network.ts` (`unknown` counts as online).
- `src/query/persister.ts`: `createSqlitePersister(storage)` over the `expo-sqlite/kv-store` API (one row `hexascore.queryCache`); `dehydrateOptions` = no mutations, success-state queries only, **allowlist** of read operation keys (`queryKey[0]` must be a `ReadClient` method on the list; `tokensIndex` is left out); 10 MB budget enforced on write by dropping the oldest queries (`dataUpdatedAt`); unparsable row removed; `cacheBuster(appVersion, formulaVersion)`; `trackFormulaVersion` stores the last seen `formulaVersion`, which feeds the buster on the next launch (the buster is fixed at launch by `PersistQueryClientProvider`).
- `src/auth/runtime.ts`: `queryClient`, `persistOptions` (`maxAge` 24 h), managers wired, `wipeUserData` now clears the in-memory cache and removes the persisted row (401, ban, logout).
- `app/_layout.tsx`: wrapped in `PersistQueryClientProvider`.
- `jest.setup.ts`: in-memory `expo-sqlite/kv-store` mock (no native SQLite under Jest).

Dependencies: `@tanstack/react-query` 5.105.0, `@tanstack/react-query-persist-client` 5.105.0 and `expo-sqlite` 58.0.15 were already in `apps/mobile/package.json` (owner yes, Q-B1). No new packages. Uses `expo-sqlite/kv-store` (shipped inside `expo-sqlite`), so no other storage library (D-22).

SDD: 10 §5 (persisted cache row: read queries only, no tokens, no problem bodies, 10 MB, 24 h, buster), §7.1, §7.3 "Reads", NFR-10-6; TanStack persistQueryClient docs [E2].
