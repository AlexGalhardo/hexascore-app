## Draft paragraphs for the owner (English, not yet published)

Terms (`/terms`, proposed):

> Some links on HexaScore go to online stores that run affiliate programmes. If you buy through such a link, HexaScore may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Where a store requires it, the link is marked as sponsored and the page says so. Links that carry an affiliate tag appear only when you are signed in to HexaScore.

Privacy (`/privacy`, proposed):

> When you follow an affiliate link, the store receives your visit and handles your data under its own privacy policy and cookies. HexaScore does not send your account details, e-mail or ratings to the store.

Both paragraphs need the owner's review and the disclosure text confirmed from each Operating Agreement before publication.
