# Google Play listing (en)

Play limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4,000 characters. `bun apps/mobile/store/listing/check.ts` checks them.

## Title

HexaScore

## Short description

Community scores for games, movies, series and books, rated in 6 categories.

## Full description

HexaScore scores every game, movie, TV series and book by what people think of it, category by category.

A single number hides too much. On HexaScore each title is rated from 0 to 100 in six categories that fit its kind: gameplay, fun, sound, technical, visuals and storytelling for games; screenplay, acting, direction, cinematography, soundtrack and entertainment for movies; plot, characters, writing, pacing, originality and impact for books. You see where a title shines and where it falls short before you spend your evening on it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Browse games, movies, series and books, with trending titles on the home screen.
- Search the whole catalogue at once.
- Open a title to see its community score and the score per category.
- Rate what you played, watched or read, one category at a time.
- Keep a library: want to, in progress, completed or dropped.
- Build your own lists.
- Read your notifications in the app.

MADE FOR THE PHONE

- Ratings and library changes made offline are saved and sent when you are back online.
- Light and dark themes.
- English, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish.
- Sign in with your email or with Google. Browsing works without an account.

ON THE WEB

Your account is the same on the app and on hexascore.app. Subscriptions and API keys for developers are managed on the website only; the app has no purchases.

PRIVACY

No ads and no tracking SDKs. Crash reports are sent only if you allow them. You can delete your account in the app or at hexascore.app/help/delete-account.

Movie and series data from TMDB. Game data from IGDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
